# Historical Portals: images, people and rights

This file explains how the photographs and portraits in the Historical Portals were chosen and checked.
The per-item evidence (offices with dates and sources, every image's license, author and source page) is
generated by `npm run media` into [history-verification.md](history-verification.md).

## Open issue before submission: competition Rule 17

The portals show **real, identifiable people** (names, offices and portraits of heads of government,
finance ministers and central bank governors) and real street scenes. Rule 17 of the Meta VR Start
Developer Competition 2026 restricts the mention or depiction of identifiable people other than the entrant.

- This has **not** been confirmed with the organizers. Nothing in this repository should be read as saying
  the app complies with Rule 17.
- Before submitting, ask the organizers whether historical public figures (and street photos that may show
  passers-by, signs or brand names) are acceptable. If they are not, the app can fall back without code
  changes: remove the people from `content/history.json` and the `portal.photos` lists, run `npm run media`,
  and the windows show the code-generated impressions (labelled "Artistic impression, not a photograph").

## How items were chosen and checked

- **People:** listed by hand in `content/history.json` with the office to check. The build script verifies
  that each person held that office on the event date: Wikidata `position held` with start/end dates, or,
  when Wikidata has no dated record of that office, the term dates in the person's English Wikipedia
  infobox. Unverified people are excluded automatically.
- **Portraits:** the image Wikidata lists for the person (property P18), so the face shown is the one the
  person's own record points to. No portrait is ever substituted: people without one get a plain plate with
  initials and "no portrait available".
- **City photos:** chosen by hand from Wikimedia Commons categories for the city and year (for example
  "1971 in Washington, D.C.", "1998 in Seoul"), looked at in full, and recorded with their caption and date.
- **Licenses:** read from each file's Commons page. Accepted: public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, KOGL
  Type 1. Not accepted: anything non-commercial (NC), no-derivatives (ND) or unknown. Credits (author,
  license, source) are shown in the window's card in the app and listed in history-verification.md.
- **Files:** optimized local WebP files under `public/media/` (portraits 288 px square, photos at most 1024 px
  wide; about 1.3 MB in total). The app never loads images from other sites at runtime. Only the textures of
  the window on screen (and the next one) are kept in GPU memory.

## Limits of the checks

- The wording of each person's involvement and of the policies is our own short summary of the public
  record. It is not verified automatically; please review it.
- Wikimedia Commons licenses are as declared on Commons. For CC BY-SA photos, the share-alike condition
  applies to the photos themselves (we cropped and re-encoded them; they remain under their license).
- Street photos may show passers-by or signs. We avoided close-ups of people in the city photos, but small,
  distant figures and shop signs are visible in some of them (see the Rule 17 note above).
